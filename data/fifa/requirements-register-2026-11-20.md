## Scope and status of this transcription

The source describes **181 checks in 11 chapters**, including **123 that apply to stadiums**. It states that most checks are working targets and that the 2034 contract clause is still to be confirmed. This file preserves that distinction; it is a transcription of the supplied register, not an independently verified statement of official FIFA contractual requirements.

- **WT** means the source says: “Working target · Threshold to be confirmed (2034 clause)”.
- **Book** means the source provides a numerical criterion with explanatory book/guidance text instead of the WT label. The source introduction says all values remain subject to the 2034 contract clause.
- **Partial** marks text obscured by the on-screen navigation. Missing wording and identifiers have not been invented.
- **Section** is the book section number visible in the screenshots. Source titles, URLs, some section names, page numbers, and the right-hand evidence column are cropped. No missing links or text have been reconstructed.
- The evidence column repeatedly shows “Evidence requested”, “calculations / tests”, and “applicable”, with intervening text cropped. These fragments are recorded here once rather than presented as a complete evidence specification for every row.

The source inventory is shown as **8 entries**, but its contents are not expanded in the screenshots. Visible source-link fragments include “FIFA Football Stadi…”, “stadiums”, “main user groups”, “pitch dimensions … areas”, “precinct and peri…”, “around the traini…”, “player and coach…”, and “FIFA World Cup 2… Hosting Require…”.
